## Final prompt

Use case: style-transfer. Edit image 1, the Bickers glass app icon, to TONED DOWN, SUBTLE GLASS, as requested by the user. Image 2 is the original B logo for exact silhouette reference. Preserve the original forward-slanted B letterform, proportions, counter shapes, position, size, black background and monochrome brand. Change the finish only. Reduce the glass intensity by about 80 percent: remove the shiny double outline, chrome-like bevels, bright rim, deep extrusion, internal ripples and strong reflections. The B should be a clean, softly frosted pale neutral-grey glass shape, mostly opaque, almost flat, with a very gentle broad light transition and a barely perceptible soft highlight along its upper edge. Think restrained satin glass, calm and contemporary. Give it a faint, very shallow soft shadow for separation from a smooth near-black graphite background. The letter face should read as a clear unified pale grey silhouette, not a dark interior surrounded by bright edges. Avoid pure-white glare, outlined counters, metallic chrome, hard bevels, pronounced 3D, realistic heavy lens refraction, texture, vivid colours and red accents. The effect should feel like 90 percent clean flat logo and 10 percent glass. No changes to the B design. Front-on orthographic, same framing as image 1. Single square app-icon master, opaque full-bleed background, square outer corners for platform masking. No phone, captions, labels, border or watermark. This should visibly be a much calmer and less glossy version of the previous icon.
